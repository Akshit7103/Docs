Each session is planned for 90 minutes: a walkthrough on the live instance, a short hands-on exercise, and time for questions. Calendar invites will follow with the meeting links.

What each session covers

  1. End-to-end demo. One settlement email from mailbox drop to matched cashflow and audit trail, so everyone sees the whole journey before we go into the parts.
  2. ServiceNow platform. Application structure and scopes, the code-as-metadata approach, repository and build, Studio, update sets and the promotion path to bsmdev, roles and access.
  3. Onboarding wizard. How a manager defines a work driver without code: identification rules, extraction fields and prompts, target mapping, matching tolerances, publishing and assignment.
  4. Email ingestion and classification. Mailbox and zip drop, parsing of body, Excel and PDF attachments, sender and counterparty derivation, routing rules and the ownership gate, the planned Exchange (EWS) connection.
  5. Email extraction and Compare and Match. The Chinou integration, prompts and guardrails, multi-source extraction and de-duplication, confidence, the three-tier matcher and its configuration.
  6. Outcomes and analyst write-back. The analyst board and case screen, confirm, override and resolution, Outlook tagging, the audit trail, observability and cost tracking, operational runbooks and the open backlog.
